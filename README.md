# SwapSkill — Learn One. Teach One. Grow Together.

## About the Project

**SwapSkill** is an inclusive, credit-based peer-to-peer skill exchange platform that connects students who want to learn with students who can teach. It enables students to share their knowledge, discover new skills, collaborate with peers, and grow together without making money the primary barrier to education.

Built for the **MISSION'26 Hackathon — Smart Education Track**, SwapSkill aims to make learning more accessible, collaborative, and community-driven.

## Key Features

- **Skill Discovery:** Explore skills across technology, design, academics, communication, business, arts, and many other domains.
- **Smart Matching:** Discover compatible learning partners based on skills, proficiency, languages, and availability.
- **Student Profiles:** Showcase skills you teach, skills you want to learn, learning goals, and experience.
- **Skill Exchange:** Start discussions, propose lessons, arrange sessions, and track their status.
- **Continuous Learning:** Create multi-lesson learning programs, monitor progress, and manage individual lessons.
- **Credit Wallet:** Earn and use platform credits through verified skill exchanges, with safeguards against duplicate rewards.
- **Flexible Payments:** Support credit-based exchanges and optional monetary payments when a provider offers them.
- **Community:** Connect with friends, exchange friend codes, share knowledge, join study groups, and participate in discussions.
- **Messaging:** Discuss skill exchanges with other students while keeping general Community chat restricted to accepted friends.
- **Calendar:** Track proposed and confirmed sessions, schedules, and meeting details.
- **Nova Assistant:** Get help discovering skills, planning learning goals and navigating SwapSkill.
- **Learning Progress:** Track goals, completed lessons, reviews, and achievements.
- **Accessibility and Personalization:** Responsive design, light and dark themes and language preferences.

## How It Works

1. Create an account and set up your profile.
2. Add skills you can teach and skills you want to learn.
3. Discover a compatible learning partner.
4. Discuss the exchange and agree on lesson details.
5. Choose an available payment method and confirm the session or learning program.
6. Attend lessons and track progress.
7. Confirm lesson completion to trigger eligible credit rewards according to the configured rules.

## Technology and Architecture

The application is designed for a modern web stack, with a responsive frontend, structured application logic, and an optional backend such as Supabase for authentication, database storage and real-time features.

The final technology stack depends on the implementation generated by the chosen development platform. External authentication, AI, and payment integrations require appropriate configuration.

## Safety and Transparency

- Separate demonstration data from real student accounts.
- Protect private profile information and enforce appropriate access controls.
- Prevent duplicate credit rewards and negative balances.
- Require both participants to verify lesson completion before eligible rewards are issued.
- Restrict Community chat to accepted friends.
- Support reporting, blocking, and privacy controls.
- Clearly label simulated payments and unconfigured integrations.

## Project Goal

SwapSkill transforms peer-to-peer learning into a collaborative experience where every student has something valuable to teach and something new to learn.
